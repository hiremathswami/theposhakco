1. WHAT ARE COOKIES

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and device identifiers. They help a website remember your actions and preferences.

2. HOW WE USE COOKIES

A. Essential
These are required for the Website to work and cannot be switched off. They are used to:
- Keep you signed in to your account
- Remember the items in your cart, wishlist, and recently viewed products
- Process checkout securely and prevent fraud

B. Preference
These remember choices you make, such as filters or region, to give you a more personal experience.

C. Analytics
These help us understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve pages, products, and performance. Data is aggregated wherever possible.

D. Marketing
If enabled, these may be used to show relevant ads on other platforms and measure campaign performance. We will request consent for non-essential cookies where required by law.

3. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Some cookies may be set by trusted service providers, such as hosting, analytics, payment, or social-media partners. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

4. MANAGING COOKIES

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Clearing browser storage will also empty a guest cart and wishlist. Blocking essential cookies may stop sign-in, cart, or checkout from working.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version.
